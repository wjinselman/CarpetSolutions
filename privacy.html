Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page explains how CarPET Friendly Solutions handles information when you visit this website or contact us for service.

Information you choose to send

If you call, text, email, or otherwise contact us, we may receive the name, phone number, email address, photos, address, and service details you choose to provide. We use that information to answer questions, prepare estimates, schedule service, and provide customer support.

Online estimate requests

Online requests and any selected photos are sent through FormSubmit to our business email. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in photos. FormSubmit states that submission details are retained in its archive for 30 days; uploaded files are not retained in that archive. Details you enter in the quote wizard are also kept temporarily in this browser tab so you can continue your request.

Website analytics

With your permission, this site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand visits and improve the website. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information about how pages are used. You can decline analytics through the analytics controls at the bottom of each page.

Third-party links

This site links to services such as Google Maps, Google Reviews, text messaging, and product or payment services. Those services have their own privacy practices, which apply when you use them.

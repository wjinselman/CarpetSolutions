Do you offer flood mitigation?

Yes. We take on water extraction, affected carpet and pad removal, cleanup, and drying support within our service scope. Tell us the source and extent of the water damage so we can discuss the work needed.

What does minor restoration include?

Our service focuses on carpet and accessible floor recovery: pad replacement, relaying suitable carpet, cleaning, and odor treatment. Structural repairs and mold remediation are outside this service.

Can you save the carpet and padding?

That depends on the water source, condition of the materials, and extent of damage. We discuss what may be recoverable and what needs replacement after reviewing the project.

How soon can the carpet go back down?

Timing depends on the condition of the carpet, backing, and floor. We plan reinstallation around readiness, rather than promising a fixed drying time.

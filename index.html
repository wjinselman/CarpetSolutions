How it works!

Our carbonated cleaning process uses millions of tiny bubbles to loosen dirt and grime from deep inside your carpet fibers. The dirt rises to the surface, where it is extracted using less water than traditional steam cleaning. With no heavy soaps or sticky residue, your carpet dries faster and stays cleaner longer.

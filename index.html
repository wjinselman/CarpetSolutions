Service Policies

Clear expectations help us provide the best possible service.

Cleaning results

We provide the highest-quality cleaning reasonably possible based on the condition of the carpet, rug, upholstery, or fabric. Permanent stains, fiber wear, bleaching, dye loss, burns, pet damage, sun fading, prior chemical damage, backing deterioration, and other pre-existing damage may not improve through cleaning.

Service concerns and refunds

Charges for completed cleaning services are generally nonrefundable once the agreed service has been performed. If you have a concern, please contact us promptly so we can inspect the area and determine whether additional treatment is appropriate.

Payment terms and card processing fee

Payment is due at the time service is completed unless other arrangements have been made in advance. A disclosed processing fee may apply to eligible credit-card payments. Fee-free payment options may include cash, check, debit card, or other stated methods.

Travel outside Ardmore

Appointments outside Ardmore may include a reasonable travel fee based on distance and service location. Any travel fee will be disclosed before the appointment is confirmed.
